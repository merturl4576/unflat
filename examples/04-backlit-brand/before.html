Getting there

Water taxi from Erasmusbrug every 15 minutes until 06:30, metro D to Maashaven, and a night bus back to Centraal on the half hour. No parking on the quay.

Age

18+, no exceptions. Bring a passport or an ID card; a photo of one is not enough at the gate.

Lockers and cloakroom

€6 a locker, €3 a coat, both on Pier 1 and Pier 2 and open all night. Anything left behind goes to the office on Monday.

Accessibility

Step-free on Pier 1 and Pier 2, with a raised viewing platform on the main stage. Pier 3 is reached by a ramped gangway. A companion ticket is free.
