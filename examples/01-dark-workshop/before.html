Three ways to make a part

Send a DXF, an SVG or a sketch on a napkin. We check tolerances, nest the parts and send a quote the same day.

Laser cutting

CO₂ laser for wood and acrylic up to 12 mm, fibre laser for steel and aluminium up to 6 mm. Clean edges, minimal char, no post-sanding on plywood.

CNC milling

Three-axis routing for pockets, chamfers and joinery the laser cannot do. Solid hardwoods, Valchromat, HPL and composite panels.

Engraving

Raster and vector engraving to 0.2 mm depth for logos, serial numbers and wayfinding. Repeatable across a batch of one or a batch of ten thousand.
